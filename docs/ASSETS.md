# Visual assets — Maison Aube 1.1

The website uses two original AI-generated concept photographs produced with the built-in image-generation tool. They depict a fictional bakery and are not photographs of a real business. The site footer and FAQ disclose this.

## Prompt set

- `assets/morning.webp`: premium editorial artisan bakery photograph; golden flaky croissant on an ivory plate, rustic sourdough loaf and espresso on oak beside a sunlit window; natural morning light, tactile pastry and bread texture; no people, lettering, logos or watermark.
- `assets/atelier.webp`: warm independent European bakery interior; ivory limewash walls, arched sunlit window, oak counter and bread shelves, small round tables and bentwood chairs; authentic architectural photography; no people, readable signage, logos or watermark.

Images are optimized as WebP without visual compositing. The original PNGs are retained in the project workspace. Final theme assets are under `theme/maison-aube/assets/`; the static preview uses matching files under `preview/assets/`.

Cormorant Garamond and DM Sans fonts are self-hosted. Their SIL Open Font License files are included alongside the font files. The WordPress theme code is GPL-2.0-or-later. AI-generated image assets are supplied for this fictional demonstration and are identified separately from third-party font licenses.
